# Lake Neuchâtel boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Neuchâtel boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go, how long they take, how often they run in summer, and which tickets and passes are valid. Lake Neuchâtel is the largest lake that lies entirely in Switzerland, and canals link it to Lake Murten and Lake Biel, so a boat from Neuchâtel can take you to Murten or Biel/Bienne. Lacus puts every pier and every boat of this network into one search, from the official timetable.

## How the boats work

The regular boats on Lake Neuchâtel are run by the Société de Navigation sur les lacs de Neuchâtel et Morat (LNM). LNM runs Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Murten as one network: its boats pass from one lake to the other through the Broye canal, and through the Thielle canal (the Zihl canal) to Lake Biel. Because of this, Lacus shows the same piers and trips for Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Murten.

The boats run from spring to autumn only. For 2026 the periods are:

| Period | Dates | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Low season | 10 April – 18 June 2026 | Friday to Sunday and public holidays |
| High season | 19 June – 27 September 2026 | Every day |
| Low season | 2 October – 18 October 2026 | Friday to Sunday and public holidays |

There are no regular LNM boats in winter. LNM's dates for 2027 were not yet on its website on 19 October 2026.

On Lake Neuchâtel, every route in the 2026 timetable starts or ends at Neuchâtel, the main port:

- A loop across the lake to Cudrefin and Portalban and back to Neuchâtel
- Along the north-west shore to Auvernier, Cortaillod, Bevaix and Estavayer-le-Lac, with the return by Chevroux
- Through the Broye canal to Sugiez and Murten/Morat, by St-Blaise and La Sauge
- Through the Thielle canal to Lake Biel, calling at Le Landeron, Erlach, La Neuveville, St. Petersinsel Nord, Ligerz and Twann on the way to Biel/Bienne

Lake Murten also has its own round trip from Murten/Morat, which the Lake Murten guide describes.

The Bielersee-Schifffahrts-Gesellschaft (BSG), which runs the boats on Lake Biel, also runs one boat a day each way between Biel/Bienne, Neuchâtel and Murten/Morat. LNM's own timetable shows it beside its own trips.

## Classic combinations

- **The three lakes:** from Neuchâtel, one boat goes through the Thielle canal to Lake Biel and another through the Broye canal to Murten, so a day can take in all three lakes. LNM's timetable groups these as its one-lake, two-lake and three-lake trips.
- **Round trip across the lake:** the boat from Neuchâtel calls at Cudrefin and Portalban, on the south shore, and comes back to Neuchâtel in 1 hour 20 minutes.
- **St. Peter's Island:** the boat from Neuchâtel to Biel/Bienne calls at St. Petersinsel Nord, on Lake Biel, about 1 hour 35 minutes from Neuchâtel.

## The boats

LNM has seven boats, including the steamer Neuchâtel, the only lake and river steamer in Switzerland:

| Boat | Built |
|---|---|
| Neuchâtel (steamer) | 1912 |
| Ville d'Estavayer | 1961 |
| Ville d'Yverdon | 1965 |
| Ville de Neuchâtel | 1972 |
| La Béroche | 1981 |
| Fribourg | 1995 |
| Idée Suisse | 2002 |

The steamer Neuchâtel ran until 1969 and then became a restaurant boat in the port of Neuchâtel. The Trivapor association bought it in 2007 and restored it, it went back on the water in 2013, and LNM has run it since 2014. It has 300 places. The Fribourg and the Ville d'Yverdon have 560 places each.

Some trips have an onboard restaurant, where a reservation is recommended, and others a bistro.

## Tickets and passes

LNM's conditions give these rules for its timetable boats:

- The GA travelcard is valid.
- Children under 6 travel free, and children from 6 to 16 pay half price.
- With the Junior Card, children from 6 to 16 travel free with a parent.
- Groups of 10 or more get 30% off.
- Online tickets are sold in first or second class, single or return, and are valid for one day.

LNM sells tickets online. Its ticket offices are in Neuchâtel and Murten. A family day card for two adults and up to four children costs CHF 111 and is sold on board and at those offices. From 65, seniors can buy a day card for CHF 35 instead of CHF 72, valid on Mondays and Fridays only.

The 3 Lakes+ season pass (Abonnement 3lacs+) is valid on all timetable boats of LNM and BSG on Lake Neuchâtel, Lake Murten, Lake Biel and the Aare. For 2026 it cost CHF 189 before 2 May and CHF 219 from 2 May. BSG's day card is also valid on LNM boats.

## Special cruises

Besides the timetable, LNM runs special cruises, many of them from Neuchâtel and some on the steamer. They continue after the timetable boats stop for the winter. Recent examples include the Halloween cruise, the Brunch cruise and the Fondue cruise, all from Neuchâtel. Lacus lists these special cruises, with their dates and prices, in the app.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. You buy your ticket from LNM, the boat operator, online on its website; Lacus links each trip to that website.

**Do the boats on Lake Neuchâtel run in winter?**
No. In 2026 the regular boats run from 10 April to 18 October, every day from 19 June to 27 September and on Fridays to Sundays and public holidays outside that. LNM's special cruises, such as the Fondue cruise, continue into December.

**How long is the boat from Neuchâtel to Murten?**
About 1 hour 35 minutes, through the Broye canal. In summer 2026 there are three direct boats a day each way, and on low-season days two.

**Can I go by boat from Neuchâtel to Biel/Bienne?**
Yes. The direct boat takes about 2 hours 25 minutes and passes through the Thielle canal and along Lake Biel. LNM runs one a day each way, and BSG runs another.

**Is the GA travelcard valid on the boats?**
Yes, on LNM's timetable boats. Children under 6 travel free and children from 6 to 16 pay half price.

**Can I take my bike on the boat?**
Yes, if there is space; there is no obligation to carry bikes. You load your bike yourself. It costs CHF 5 for one stop, or CHF 8 for a bike day card (CHF 5 for children). Folding bikes and bikes in a bag travel free, and the SBB bike pass is valid.

**Do I need to reserve a table for the restaurant?**
LNM recommends a reservation for its onboard restaurants and asks individual travellers to reserve their table online, by e-mail or by phone.
