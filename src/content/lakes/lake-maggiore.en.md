# Lake Maggiore boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Maggiore boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go on the Swiss end of the lake, how long they take, how often they run in summer and in winter, and how tickets work. Lake Maggiore is a long lake shared by Switzerland and Italy, and only its northern end is Swiss. Lacus covers the boats of that Swiss end, around Locarno, Ascona and the Brissago Islands, plus the Italian town of Cannobio, which the same boats reach. The larger Italian network further south, with Stresa and the Borromean Islands, is not in Lacus.

## How the boats work

On the Swiss end of Lake Maggiore, the boats in Lacus are run by the Società Navigazione del Lago di Lugano (SNL), the Lake Lugano Navigation Company, which publishes their timetables on its Lake Locarno site. It runs two services:

- **Line 351, Locarno–Magadino:** a short line across the top of the lake. It runs all year, from early morning to early evening, and is part of the Arcobaleno fare network (Comunità tariffale Arcobaleno). In the summer timetable some boats also call at Tenero.
- **The tourist line:** boats from Locarno to Ascona, the Brissago Islands and Brissago, with some trips calling at San Nazzaro, Vira, Gerra and Porto Ronco. In 2026 it runs every day from 18 March to 8 November.

The timetable of line 351 changes three times a year. For 2025–26 the periods are:

| Period | Dates |
|---|---|
| Winter | 14 December 2025 – 28 March 2026 |
| Summer (with Tenero) | 29 March – 18 October 2026 |
| Autumn and winter | 19 October – 12 December 2026 |

The tourist line has its own shorter timetables at the end of the season: one from 19 October to 1 November 2026, and one from 2 to 8 November 2026, with three boats a day from Locarno to Brissago.

On Tuesdays and Sundays from 29 March to 8 November 2026, a few boats go on from Brissago over the border to Cannobio, in Italy.

The Italian company Navigazione Laghi runs the boats on the Italian part of the lake, and some of its boats also come up to Brissago, Ascona and Locarno. Those boats are not in Lacus.

## Classic combinations

- **The Brissago Islands:** the islands off Brissago hold a botanical garden. It is open every day from 18 March to 8 November 2026, the same days the tourist boats run, and the islands are about 20 minutes by boat from Ascona.
- **Locarno and Ascona:** the two towns are linked by the tourist line, with stops at San Nazzaro, Vira and Gerra on some trips.
- **Cannobio:** on Tuesdays and Sundays, the boats from Brissago and Locarno go over the border to Cannobio, in Italy.

## The boats

SNL runs the boats on both Lake Lugano and the Swiss end of Lake Maggiore. Its fleet page lists 13 boats in all, from the small electric Vedetta of 1908 to the San Gottardo of 2001, but it does not say which of them run on Lake Maggiore. Lacus shows the name of the boat for a trip only where the operator publishes it, and for Lake Maggiore it does not show one.

## Tickets and passes

Line 351, Locarno–Magadino, is part of the Arcobaleno fare network. SNL sells tickets for its Lake Maggiore boats online. Bicycles cannot be taken on line 351 between Locarno and Tenero.

The tourist line and the boats to Cannobio cross into Italy, and SNL's pages do not say which travelcards and passes are valid on them. Check the fare for your trip in SNL's shop before you travel.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. You buy your ticket from SNL, the boat operator, online; Lacus links each trip to SNL's timetable and ticket page.

**Does Lacus show the boats to Stresa and the Borromean Islands?**
No. Lacus covers only SNL's boats on the Swiss end of the lake, plus Cannobio. The boats on the Italian part of the lake, to Stresa and the Borromean Islands, are run by Navigazione Laghi and are not in Lacus.

**How do I get to the Brissago Islands by boat?**
Take the tourist line from Locarno, Ascona or Brissago. From Ascona it takes about 15 minutes, with 8 direct boats on a summer weekday. From Locarno it takes 45 to 65 minutes, with 5 direct boats. The boats and the garden run from 18 March to 8 November 2026.

**Do the boats on Lake Maggiore run in winter?**
Partly. Line 351 between Locarno and Magadino runs all year, with 12 boats a day each way on a winter weekday. The tourist line to Ascona and the Brissago Islands stops after 8 November and does not run in the winter timetable.

**How long is the boat from Locarno to Ascona?**
About 30 to 50 minutes, depending on the stops. Most boats take about 35 minutes, calling at San Nazzaro, and some also at Vira and Gerra. On a summer weekday there are 5 direct boats.

**Can I take the boat to Cannobio?**
Yes, on Tuesdays and Sundays from 29 March to 8 November 2026. One direct boat a day leaves Locarno at 09:40, and three go from Brissago, taking about 20 minutes.

**Can I take my bike on the boat?**
Not on line 351 between Locarno and Tenero, where bicycles are not carried. SNL's timetables do not give a rule for the other trips.
