# Lake Murten boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Murten boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go, how long they take, how often they run in summer, and which tickets and passes are valid. Lake Murten, the Lac de Morat, is the smallest of the three lakes at the foot of the Jura, and the Broye canal links it to Lake Neuchâtel. Its boats go round the lake from the walled town of Murten and through the canal to Neuchâtel. Lacus puts every pier and every boat of this network into one search, from the official timetable.

## How the boats work

The regular boats on Lake Murten are run by the Société de Navigation sur les lacs de Neuchâtel et Morat (LNM), which runs Lake Murten and Lake Neuchâtel as one network. Its boats pass between the two lakes through the Broye canal. Because of this, Lacus shows the same piers and trips for Lake Murten and Lake Neuchâtel.

The boats run from spring to autumn only. For 2026 the periods are:

| Period | Dates | Days |
|---|---|---|
| Low season | 10 April – 18 June 2026 | Friday to Sunday and public holidays |
| High season | 19 June – 27 September 2026 | Every day |
| Low season | 2 October – 18 October 2026 | Friday to Sunday and public holidays |

There are no regular LNM boats in winter. LNM's dates for 2027 were not yet on its website on 19 October 2026.

The boats leave from Murten/Morat. In the 2026 timetable there are two kinds of route:

- A round trip of the lake: Murten/Morat, Praz, Môtier (Vully), Vallamand, Faoug and back to Murten/Morat, in 1 hour 15 minutes
- Through the Broye canal to Neuchâtel, calling at Sugiez, Trois-Lacs and La Sauge, and on some boats at Praz and Môtier (Vully)

The Bielersee-Schifffahrts-Gesellschaft (BSG), which runs the boats on Lake Biel, also runs one boat a day from Murten/Morat through Lake Neuchâtel to Lake Biel and Biel/Bienne, and one back. BSG calls it the three-lakes trip.

## Classic combinations

- **Round the lake:** the round trip from Murten/Morat calls at the villages of the Vully and at Faoug, and brings you back to Murten/Morat in 1 hour 15 minutes.
- **Two lakes:** the boat from Murten/Morat goes up the Broye canal and across Lake Neuchâtel to Neuchâtel in about 1 hour 40 minutes. LNM's timetable calls these its two-lake trips.
- **The three lakes:** BSG's three-lakes trip runs between Murten/Morat and Biel/Bienne through the Broye and Zihl canals. From Biel/Bienne the trip to Murten takes about 4 hours 10 minutes, and the boat stops in Murten for almost an hour before it goes back to Neuchâtel and Biel/Bienne.

## Tickets and passes

LNM's conditions give these rules for its timetable boats:

- The GA travelcard is valid.
- Children under 6 travel free, and children from 6 to 16 pay half price.
- With the Junior Card, children from 6 to 16 travel free with a parent.
- Groups of 10 or more get 30% off.
- Online tickets are sold in first or second class, single or return, and are valid for one day.

LNM sells tickets online, and it has ticket offices in Murten and Neuchâtel. A family day card for two adults and up to four children costs CHF 111 and is sold on board and at those offices. From 65, seniors can buy a day card for CHF 35 instead of CHF 72, valid on Mondays and Fridays only.

The 3 Lakes+ season pass (Abonnement 3lacs+) is valid on all timetable boats of LNM and BSG on Lake Murten, Lake Neuchâtel, Lake Biel and the Aare. For 2026 it cost CHF 189 before 2 May and CHF 219 from 2 May.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. You buy your ticket from LNM, the boat operator, online on its website; Lacus links each trip to that website.

**Do the boats on Lake Murten run in winter?**
No. In 2026 the regular boats run from 10 April to 18 October, every day from 19 June to 27 September and on Fridays to Sundays and public holidays outside that.

**How long is the boat from Murten to Neuchâtel?**
About 1 hour 40 minutes, through the Broye canal. In summer 2026 there are three direct boats a day each way, and on low-season days two.

**How long is the round trip of Lake Murten?**
1 hour 15 minutes from Murten/Morat back to Murten/Morat, with stops at Praz, Môtier (Vully), Vallamand and Faoug.

**Can I go by boat from Murten to Biel/Bienne?**
Yes, on BSG's three-lakes trip, one boat a day each way in season. It goes through Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Biel; from Biel/Bienne to Murten it takes about 4 hours 10 minutes.

**Is the GA travelcard valid on the boats?**
Yes, on LNM's timetable boats. Children under 6 travel free and children from 6 to 16 pay half price.

**Can I take my bike on the boat?**
Yes, if there is space; there is no obligation to carry bikes. You load your bike yourself. It costs CHF 5 for one stop, or CHF 8 for a bike day card (CHF 5 for children). Folding bikes and bikes in a bag travel free, and the SBB bike pass is valid.
