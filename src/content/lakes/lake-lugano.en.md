# Lake Lugano boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Lugano boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go, how long they take, how often they run in summer and in winter, and which tickets and passes are valid. Lake Lugano, the Lago di Lugano or Ceresio, winds between Switzerland and Italy, and its boats link the city of Lugano with lakeside villages such as Gandria and Morcote and with Italian towns such as Porto Ceresio and Porlezza. Lacus puts every pier and every boat in the official timetable into one search, on the Swiss and the Italian shores.

## How the boats work

The regular boats on Lake Lugano are run by one company, the Società Navigazione del Lago di Lugano (SNL), the Lake Lugano Navigation Company. It has run boats on the lake since 1848.

SNL divides the lake into two basins, each with its own routes from Lugano:

- **South basin:** Lugano–Morcote–Porto Ceresio–Lugano, calling at Paradiso, Campione d'Italia, Melide Swissminiatur, Brusino Arsizio and other piers. On Wednesdays and Saturdays, two boats a day go on to Ponte Tresa.
- **North basin:** Lugano–Gandria–Lugano, with calls at piers for the grotto restaurants and the Swiss Customs Museum. On Tuesdays and Sundays, two boats a day go on to Porlezza, at the Italian end of the lake.

In Lugano, the boats leave from Lugano Centrale, and most call at Paradiso a few minutes later.

The boats do not run all year. For 2026 the periods are:

## Classic combinations

- **Gandria:** the old fishing village at the end of the north basin route from Lugano. Its boats also call at piers for the grotto restaurants and the Swiss Customs Museum.
- **Morcote:** a lakeside village on the south basin, with its patrician houses and the church of Santa Maria del Sasso.
- **Swissminiatur:** the miniature Switzerland park at Melide has its own pier, Melide Swissminiatur, and the park suggests combining a visit with a boat trip.
- **Giro Grotti:** an evening round trip from Lugano to the grotto restaurants of the north basin, from Thursday to Saturday between 28 May and 29 August 2026. It costs CHF 20 for adults and CHF 10 for children aged 6 to 15.

## Tickets and passes

SNL sells tickets online in its shop and at its ticket offices at Lugano Centrale, Lugano Giardino, Paradiso, Gandria and Morcote. Its administrative office in Lugano sells no tickets.

The GA travelcard is only partly accepted on Lake Lugano: SBB lists it among the lakes where the GA gives a discount rather than free travel on some trips. Switzerland Tourism says the Lugano–Gandria–Lugano route is fully covered by the Swiss Travel Pass, the Swiss Travel Pass Flex and the GA. For trips into Italy, check the fare in SNL's shop before you travel.

Besides the timetable, SNL runs events on board, such as theme dinners on New Year's Eve and on 1 August, the Swiss National Day.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. You buy your ticket from SNL, the boat operator, in its online shop; Lacus links each trip to that shop, already filled in with the trip.

**Do the boats on Lake Lugano run in winter?**
Hardly. The 2026 timetable ends on 8 November, and there are no boats from then until at least 12 December 2026, where the timetable Lacus has ends. Last winter, a few round trips ran on Sundays and holidays between 14 December 2025 and 6 January 2026, and boats ran again from 28 February 2026.

**How long is the boat from Lugano to Gandria?**
About 30 to 50 minutes, depending on the stops. Most boats take about 35 minutes. On a summer weekday there are 7 direct boats from Lugano Centrale.

**How long is the boat from Lugano to Morcote?**
About 50 minutes to 1 hour, with stops such as Paradiso, Campione and Melide Swissminiatur on the way. On a summer weekday there are 6 direct boats.

**Is the Lake Lugano boat included in the Swiss Travel Pass?**
On the Lugano–Gandria–Lugano route, yes, according to Switzerland Tourism. The GA is only partly accepted on Lake Lugano, with a discount instead of free travel on some trips.

**Which days do the boats go to Porlezza and Ponte Tresa?**
Boats to Porlezza run on Tuesdays and Sundays, and boats to Ponte Tresa on Wednesdays and Saturdays, in the main season from spring to mid-October. Each has two boats a day.

**What is the Giro Grotti?**
An evening boat from Lugano to the grotto restaurants on the north basin and back, from Thursday to Saturday between 28 May and 29 August 2026. Adults pay CHF 20 and children aged 6 to 15 pay CHF 10.
