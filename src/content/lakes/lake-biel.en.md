# Lake Biel boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Biel boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go, how long they take, how often they run in summer and in winter, and which tickets and passes are valid. Lake Biel, the Bielersee or Lac de Bienne, lies on the language border between German- and French-speaking Switzerland, with vineyards along its northern shore and St. Peter's Island. Its boats also go along the Aare to Solothurn and through the canals to Neuchâtel and Murten. Lacus puts every pier and every boat on the lake into one search, from the official timetable.

## How the boats work

The boats on Lake Biel are run by the Bielersee-Schifffahrts-Gesellschaft (BSG). Its network runs from Solothurn to Murten, along what BSG calls the longest navigable waterway in Switzerland: on the Aare from Solothurn to Lake Biel, then through the Zihl canal to Lake Neuchâtel and the Broye canal to Lake Murten.

BSG has four routes:

- **Lake Biel:** Biel/Bienne to Erlach along the northern shore, calling at Tüscherz, Engelberg-Wingreis, Twann, Ligerz, St. Petersinsel Nord and La Neuveville. One way takes about 1 hour 20 minutes.
- **Aare:** Biel/Bienne to Solothurn on the river, by Nidau, Port, Brügg, Büren, Grenchen and Altreu. One way takes about 2 hours 45 minutes.
- **Two lakes:** Biel/Bienne to Neuchâtel, about 2 hours 30 minutes.
- **Three lakes:** Biel/Bienne to Murten/Morat through Lake Neuchâtel, about 4 hours 10 minutes.

The Société de Navigation sur les lacs de Neuchâtel et Morat (LNM), which runs the boats on Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Murten, also has one boat a day each way between Neuchâtel and Biel/Bienne in its season. In Lacus it is in the Lake Neuchâtel search.

## Classic combinations

- **St. Peter's Island:** take the boat from Biel/Bienne or from Erlach to St. Petersinsel Nord. The island is a nature reserve, where Jean-Jacques Rousseau once stayed, with beaches and barbecue spots. It is open all year, and you can also reach it on foot or by bike from Erlach; the path is wheelchair accessible. The hotel, restaurant, self-service and kiosk on the island are closed until 30 April 2027.
- **The Aare to Solothurn:** the river trip passes nature reserves, the storks of Altreu and the old town of Büren an der Aare with its wooden bridge, and goes through the lock at Port.
- **Three lakes:** from Biel/Bienne through the Zihl canal, across Lake Neuchâtel and up the Broye canal to Murten/Morat. The boat stops in Murten for almost an hour, then returns to Neuchâtel and Biel/Bienne.
- **The Twann gorge:** the Twannbachschlucht walk above Twann is closed from 21 August 2026 until spring 2027.

## Tickets and passes

On BSG's timetable boats, these Swiss passes and tickets are valid: the GA travelcard, the Half Fare travelcard, SBB day passes, the Junior Card and Children's co-travel card, the GA Night, the dog day pass and municipal day passes. Libero tickets and passes, and the SBB bike pass, bike day pass and bike tickets, are not valid.

BSG's own fares:

- Adults from 25 pay the full fare, and young people from 16 to 24 get 20% off.
- Children from 6 to 15 pay half price, and children under 6 travel free.
- Groups of 10 or more get 30% off.

BSG's day card costs CHF 78, or CHF 56 with the Half Fare travelcard, CHF 15 for children and CHF 111 for a family (two adults and up to four children). It is sold on every BSG boat and at the ticket office at the Biel pier, and it is also valid on LNM boats.

The 3 Lakes+ season pass is valid on all timetable boats of BSG and LNM on the Aare, Lake Biel, Lake Neuchâtel and Lake Murten. For 2026 it cost CHF 189 before 2 May and CHF 219 from 2 May. It is not valid on themed events or chartered boats.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. You buy your ticket from BSG, the boat operator; Lacus links each trip to BSG's timetable page on its website.

**Do the boats on Lake Biel run in winter?**
Only on Sundays. From 26 October to 6 December 2026 and from 28 February to 9 April 2027, two boats each Sunday go from Biel/Bienne to Erlach and two come back, calling at St. Petersinsel Nord. There are no regular boats from 7 December 2026 to 27 February 2027.

**How do I get to St. Peter's Island by boat?**
Take a boat from Biel/Bienne to St. Petersinsel Nord, about 55 minutes, or from Erlach, about 25 minutes. In summer there are five boats a day each way.

**How long is the boat from Biel to Solothurn?**
About 2 hours 45 minutes on the Aare. In summer there are two boats a day each way, but only one on Mondays.

**Can I go by boat from Biel to Murten?**
Yes, on BSG's three-lakes trip, which takes about 4 hours 10 minutes. It runs once a day each way from spring to autumn.

**Is the GA travelcard valid on the boats?**
Yes, on BSG's timetable boats. The Half Fare travelcard and the Junior Card are valid too.

**Can I take my dog on the boat?**
Yes. Dogs pay half price, and dogs under 30 cm at the shoulder travel free in a suitable container. The SBB dog day pass is valid.

**Can I take my bike on the boat?**
Yes, if there are few enough passengers and enough space. Bikes cannot be reserved, and prams and wheelchairs go first when boats are busy. SBB bike passes and tickets are not valid, but BSG sells its own bike day card.
