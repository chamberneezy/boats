# Lake Zurich boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Zurich boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go, how long they take, how often they run in summer and in winter, and which tickets and passes are valid. Lake Zurich, the Zürichsee, is a long, narrow lake that stretches from the city of Zurich to Rapperswil and the upper lake beyond, and its boats link the city with the villages on both shores, the island of Ufenau and the river Limmat. Lacus puts every pier and every boat on the lake into one search, from the official timetable.

## How the boats work

The regular boats on Lake Zurich and on the Limmat are run by one company, the Zürichsee-Schifffahrtsgesellschaft (ZSG), the Lake Zurich Navigation Company. It has 17 boats, calls at 31 piers and runs every day of the year. ZSG is part of the Zurich transport network (ZVV), so its boats use ordinary ZVV zone tickets.

The timetable changes with the seasons. For 2026 the periods are:

| Period | Dates |
|---|---|
| Winter | 14 December 2025 – 2 April 2026 |
| Spring | 3 – 30 April 2026 |
| Summer | 1 May – 30 September 2026 |
| Autumn | 1 – 18 October 2026 |
| Winter | 19 October – 12 December 2026 |

In Zurich, the lake boats leave from Bürkliplatz, which has several numbered piers. ZSG's main routes are:

- Round trips from Bürkliplatz: the mini and short lake cruises (about 1 to 1½ hours), and the long lake cruise (about 4½ hours) to Rapperswil and back, calling at piers on both shores.
- From Rapperswil: the lake cruise (about 2 hours), the upper lake cruise to Lachen and Schmerikon on weekends and public holidays, and the shuttle to the island of Ufenau.
- Cross-lake shuttles between Wädenswil, Männedorf and Stäfa, and between Thalwil, Erlenbach and Küsnacht.
- The river cruise on the Limmat, a round trip of about an hour from the Landesmuseum.

A car ferry between Horgen and Meilen is run by a separate company, Zürichsee-Fähre Horgen-Meilen AG. It also takes foot passengers and bicycles, and the trip takes about 10 minutes. In Lacus its piers are Horgen Autoquai and Meilen Autoquai. Only some of its departures are in the official timetable data, so Lacus shows fewer ferries than actually run.

### The low-water timetable

Since 19 October 2026, ZSG has run a reduced low-water timetable, with no end date announced. Küsnacht and Erlenbach are not served. The Thalwil boat calls at Küsnacht Goldbach instead of Küsnacht. The short lake cruise runs only between Bürkliplatz and Kilchberg. The long lake cruise is replaced by a round trip of about 1¾ hours from Bürkliplatz, with a stop at either Horgen or Meilen. The Wädenswil–Stäfa shuttle runs as normal. Because of steep ramps, ZSG advises against bringing wheelchairs or pushchairs on its boats while it applies.

Lacus shows only the trips that run under the low-water timetable. The official timetable data holds only the outward part of the new round trip, so Lacus shows the boat from Bürkliplatz to Horgen or Meilen but not the way back.

## Classic combinations

- **Rapperswil and the children's zoo:** Knie's children's zoo is about a ten-minute walk from the Rapperswil pier. If you buy the zoo admission at the Bürkliplatz ticket office or on the boat, you pay the group price.
- **Ufenau:** the island near Rapperswil has its own pier. The shuttle from Rapperswil takes about 15 minutes, and in summer boats also run from Pfäffikon.
- **The Limmat and the lake:** the river cruise starts at the Landesmuseum and runs down the Limmat to Bürkliplatz and the lake before returning.
- **Zürich Card:** for a stay of one to three days, the Zürich Card includes the mini lake cruise, the short lake cruise or a river cruise on a Limmat boat, along with trams, buses and trains in the city and region.

## The boats

ZSG has two paddle steamers from the Belle Époque, both built by Escher Wyss:

| Steamer | Built |
|---|---|
| Stadt Zürich | 1909 |
| Stadt Rapperswil | 1914 |

The Stadt Zürich is ZSG's oldest boat. Both steamers were kept in service thanks to the society Aktion pro Raddampfer, founded in 1970, and both still run regularly. The steamers also run regular timetable trips. In the second half of September 2026, ZSG's published boat plan put the Stadt Zürich on the long lake cruise from Bürkliplatz. From 1 to 23 October 2026, the end of the published plan, motor ships run every regular trip.

The motor ships include the Helvetia (1964), ZSG's flagship, the Linth (1952), the first three-deck motor ship in Switzerland, the Panta Rhei (2007), built for use all year, and the Albis-class boats. The Uetliberg has been converted to electric power, and its two sister ships are to follow. Lacus shows the name of the boat for each trip when ZSG publishes it, usually four to five days ahead.

## Tickets and passes

ZSG is part of the Zurich transport network (ZVV). On all scheduled routes these are valid:

- ZVV zone tickets for the zones you travel through
- GA travelcard
- Junior travelcard and Children's co-travel card
- Swiss Travel Pass and Swiss Travel Pass Flex

Children aged 6 and over, holders of the Half Fare travelcard and dogs pay the reduced fare. For example, the mini lake cruise from Bürkliplatz needs a single ticket for zones 110 and 140: CHF 7.20 in second class, or CHF 3.60 reduced. The long lake cruise needs a ZVV 9 o'clock day pass for all zones: CHF 28.00 in second class or CHF 46.20 in first. First class is available only on the long lake cruises.

ZVV tickets are sold online in the ZVV ticket shop and app, and at ZSG's ticket office at Bürkliplatz. An e-ticket must be bought or activated before you board. Since 2025, ZSG's lake cruises can also be bought in the ZVV app, under "Zürichsee Schifffahrt ZSG".

Themed cruises are different: the Cheese Fondue Cruise, for example, needs a special ZSG ticket, and the GA and Half Fare travelcard give no discount on it.

## Special cruises

Besides the timetable, ZSG runs themed cruises it calls Unique Cruises, such as the Cheese Fondue Cruise, the Fondue Chinoise Cruise, the Brunch Cruise, the Santa Claus Cruise and the New Year's Eve Cruise. The Cheese Fondue Cruise leaves Bürkliplatz at 19:30 and returns at 21:45, with fondue or raclette as much as you like, from CHF 75 for adults. In the app, Lacus shows the special trips that appear in ZSG's published boat plan, with their dates, times and boats; the themed cruises above are not always among them.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. ZSG's boats use ZVV fares, so you buy a ZVV ticket online, for example in ZVV's ticket shop or app. Lacus links each trip to ZVV's timetable, already filled in with the trip, where you can buy the matching ticket.

**Is the Lake Zurich boat included in the Swiss Travel Pass?**
Yes. The Swiss Travel Pass and the Swiss Travel Pass Flex are valid on ZSG's scheduled routes, including the trip from Zurich to Rapperswil and back. Themed cruises such as the Cheese Fondue Cruise need a special ZSG ticket.

**Is the boat included in the GA travelcard or the Half Fare travelcard?**
The GA is valid on all scheduled routes. With the Half Fare travelcard you pay the reduced fare.

**Do the boats on Lake Zurich run in winter?**
Yes. ZSG runs boats every day of the year, with a smaller winter timetable from 19 October to 12 December 2026 and, since 19 October, the low-water timetable. On a winter weekday there are 14 boats from Wädenswil to Stäfa and 14 from Thalwil to Küsnacht Goldbach, but no direct boat from Bürkliplatz to Rapperswil.

**How long is the boat from Zurich to Rapperswil?**
About 2 hours 10 minutes on a direct boat from Bürkliplatz, with stops on both shores. In the summer 2026 timetable there are 7 direct boats a day. Under the low-water timetable there are none.

**Can I reserve a table on board?**
Yes, if you order a meal. A table can be reserved only together with a food order, and for the same day only by phone, up to two hours before departure.

**Can I take my dog on the boat?**
Yes. Dogs and small pets up to 30 cm at the shoulder travel free in a basket or suitable container. Other dogs pay the reduced second-class fare. Dogs travel free on the themed cruises.

**Can I take my bike on the boat?**
Only if there is space, because ZSG cannot guarantee to carry bicycles. You need an extra ZVV ticket (second class, reduced fare) or an SBB bike day pass. Bicycles are not allowed on the Limmat boats.
