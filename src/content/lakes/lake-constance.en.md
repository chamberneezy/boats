# Lake Constance boat trips and timetable

This guide covers Lake Constance boat trips and the timetable: where the boats go, how long they take, how often they run in summer and in winter, and which tickets and passes are valid. Lake Constance, the Bodensee, is shared by Switzerland, Germany and Austria, and its regular boats link Konstanz, Meersburg, the island of Mainau, Lindau, Bregenz and the Swiss shore from Kreuzlingen to Rorschach. Lacus puts the lake's regular timetable boats and the Friedrichshafen–Romanshorn ferry into one search, from the official timetable.

## How the boats work

Several companies run the boats, and they work together as the VSU, the association of shipping companies on Lake Constance and the Rhine:

- **Bodensee-Schiffsbetriebe (BSB)**, based in Konstanz, Germany, runs most trips on the German shore, from Konstanz and Überlingen to Lindau and Bregenz, and on the Untersee between Radolfzell and Reichenau. Its fleet is 14 ships.
- **Vorarlberg Lines**, based in Bregenz, Austria, runs trips from Bregenz along the German shore to Meersburg, Mainau and Konstanz.
- **Schweizerische Bodensee-Schifffahrt (SBS)**, based in Romanshorn, runs the Swiss shore from Rorschach via Romanshorn and Kreuzlingen to Meersburg, and the routes across the lake to Lindau, Langenargen, Immenstaad and Hagnau.
- BSB and SBS together run the **car ferry between Friedrichshafen and Romanshorn**, which also takes foot passengers and bicycles.

BSB and Vorarlberg Lines give their prices in euros, SBS in Swiss francs.

The longest route runs along the German shore from Konstanz to Bregenz, calling at Meersburg, Hagnau, Immenstaad, Friedrichshafen, Langenargen, Kressbronn, Nonnenhorn, Wasserburg and Lindau. Boats on the Überlinger See link Konstanz with Meersburg, Mainau, Unteruhldingen, Dingelsdorf and Überlingen. SBS boats follow the Swiss shore from Kreuzlingen via Romanshorn and Arbon to Rorschach, and cross to Lindau and Langenargen. In 2026, BSB suspended its Untersee boats between Radolfzell and Reichenau until further notice because of low water.

Two other services on the lake are not in the timetable data Lacus uses. The car ferry between Konstanz-Staad and Meersburg, run by Stadtwerke Konstanz, takes about 15 minutes and runs all year. Boats from Kreuzlingen and Konstanz along the Untersee and the Rhine to Stein am Rhein and Schaffhausen are run by the Schweizerische Schifffahrtsgesellschaft Untersee und Rhein (URh).

## Classic combinations

- **Mainau:** the flower island has its own pier, with boats from Konstanz, Meersburg and Unteruhldingen. BSB sells combined tickets with the boat there and back and entry to the island, from Easter to October. Bicycles cannot be taken to Mainau.
- **Meersburg and Unteruhldingen:** BSB's combined tickets also cover Burg Meersburg and the Pfahlbauten museum of lake dwellings at Unteruhldingen.
- **Pfänder:** from Bregenz, BSB's combined tickets also include the Pfänderbahn, the cable car up the Pfänder mountain.
- **Across the border:** the ferry and the SBS boats link Switzerland with Germany and Austria. For any trip to Germany or Austria, take a valid passport or identity card, for children too.

## The boats

The VSU's printed timetable lists the ships of BSB, Vorarlberg Lines and SBS. BSB's include the Stuttgart, the München and the Überlingen, each for about 1,000 passengers, and the Graf Zeppelin, which has a lift between its decks. Vorarlberg Lines runs the Sonnenkönigin, the Vorarlberg and the Austria, its largest ship, for 1,200 passengers. SBS's ships include the St. Gallen, the Thurgau and the Zürich, which have galley kitchens and run between Meersburg, Kreuzlingen, Romanshorn, Rorschach and Lindau. SBS also owns the ferries Romanshorn and Euregia.

The regular timetable boats are motor ships. BSB replaced its last steamers with motor ships in 1967. The paddle steamer Hohentwiel, launched in Friedrichshafen on 11 January 1913, is the only one on the lake. It is run separately by Historische Schifffahrt Bodensee, based in Hard in Austria, for its own excursions, not on the timetable.

BSB does not publish which ship runs which trip, because ships change at short notice for charters and operational reasons.

## Tickets and passes

Each company sells its own tickets, and there is no single fare for the whole lake.

- **German and Austrian boats (BSB, Vorarlberg Lines):** on Konstanz–Bregenz and Konstanz–Überlingen there are zone day tickets that let you get on and off as often as you like on the day. Tickets bought online are valid all day, not only on the boat you chose. Single tickets on these routes do not allow a break in the trip.
- **Swiss boats (SBS):** the SBS day ticket is valid on all SBS routes, but not on the ferry or on themed trips.
- **Children:** under 6 travel free and children aged 6 to 15 pay half price. The Bodensee children's day card covers all children of a family travelling with a parent or grandparent. There are no discounts for seniors.

Swiss travelcards are only partly valid. SBB lists Lake Constance among the lakes where the GA travelcard is "only partially accepted". According to SBS, the GA, day GA and municipal day tickets give free travel on the Swiss shore as far as Kreuzlingen and on the ferry, and 50% off to Mainau, Meersburg, Bregenz, Lindau, Wasserburg, Langenargen and the routes to Hagnau and Immenstaad. The Half Fare travelcard gives 50% off all SBS boats and the ferry. The Junior Card is valid on all SBS routes. GA and Half Fare are not valid on SBS themed trips.

Regional passes: the Bodensee Ticket for train, bus and ferry gives free travel on the ferry and 25% off SBS's boats. The VSU Saison-Card is a season pass for the whole lake and the Rhine, except the two car ferries. The Deutschland-Ticket is not valid on BSB boats, on any VSU boat or on the Friedrichshafen–Romanshorn ferry.

You can buy tickets on board. On BSB boats you can pay in cash, and by card only where there is mobile reception. On SBS boats and at the Romanshorn counter you can also pay with TWINT. BSB sells tickets online, and SBS sells ferry tickets and themed trips in its online shop.

## Special cruises

Besides the timetable, Vorarlberg Lines runs special cruises such as brunches, dinners and New Year's Eve evenings. ## Frequently asked questions

**Can I buy a ticket in Lacus?**
No. Lacus doesn't sell tickets or anything else. You buy your ticket from the boat operator: online from BSB (Bodensee-Schiffsbetriebe), or on board. Lacus links each trip to BSB's timetable page, where you find the trip and buy the ticket; the trip is not filled in for you.

**Do I need to book a Lake Constance boat trip in advance?**
No. You can buy your ticket on board or at the pier. Seats are not normally reserved on the regular boats. Groups of 10 or more should register in advance.

**Is the boat included in the GA travelcard or the Half Fare travelcard?**
Partly. According to SBS, the GA gives free travel on the Swiss shore as far as Kreuzlingen and on the Friedrichshafen–Romanshorn ferry, and 50% off trips to Mainau, Meersburg, Lindau and Bregenz. The Half Fare travelcard gives 50% off SBS boats and the ferry.

**Do the boats on Lake Constance run in winter?**
Only the Friedrichshafen–Romanshorn ferry runs all year. In 2026 the regular boats stopped on 18 October. On a winter weekday the ferry leaves Romanshorn 16 times, every hour from 05:22 to 20:22.

**How long is the boat from Konstanz to Mainau?**
About 30 minutes on the fastest boat. In summer 2026 there were 10 direct boats a day; six of them call at Meersburg on the way and take about 1 hour.

**How long is the boat from Konstanz to Bregenz?**
About 3 h 40 min on a direct boat, which calls at piers along the whole German shore. In peak season 2026 there were 2 direct boats a day.

**Do I need a passport?**
Yes, for any trip to Germany or Austria take a valid passport or identity card, for children too.

**Can I take my dog or my bike on the boat?**
Dogs travel free on BSB and SBS regular boats and the ferry, but not on themed trips. Bikes need a bike ticket and go if there is space; they cannot be taken to Mainau.
