Boat timetables for every Swiss lake

Lacus puts the boats of the twelve big Swiss lakes in one place: every public boat and every pier, which boat runs each trip where the operator publishes it, and special cruises such as fondue, brunch and full-moon evenings. Times are Swiss local time. Tickets are bought from each lake's boat company.

Lacus is coming soon as a free app for iPhone and Android, with no account needed.

The twelve lakes

  • Lake Lucerne (Vierwaldstättersee)
  • Lake Geneva (Lac Léman)
  • Lake Zurich (Zürichsee)
  • Lake Constance (Bodensee)
  • Lake Neuchâtel (Lac de Neuchâtel)
  • Lake Lugano (Lago di Lugano)
  • Lake Maggiore (Lago Maggiore)
  • Lake Thun (Thunersee)
  • Lake Brienz (Brienzersee)
  • Lake Zug (Zugersee)
  • Lake Murten (Murtensee / Lac de Morat)
  • Lake Biel (Bielersee / Lac de Bienne)
